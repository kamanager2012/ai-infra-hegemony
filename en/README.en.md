## Thesis

This is a margin monopoly, not a share monopoly. Prepayments lock the next node. The increment sits in HPC. What is left over goes to phones and PCs. China's stack is three layers, not one; at home it can at most supply phones in parallel. Public next-gen cloud ASICs still use advanced packaging and HBM. They have not retreated to commodity DRAM. Power is the third quota. Antitrust inquiries exist. Complaints do not.
