## Core mechanism map

The report's recurring models are:

- **AI Resource Gravity** — higher-value AI demand attracts scarce manufacturing and infrastructure resources.
- **Super-buyer Effect** — order scale, credit, and long-duration demand change allocation and financing.
- **AHICR** — the share of **incremental** advanced HPC capacity captured by the head player.
- **Non-AI Residual Capacity** — effective supply left after AI and other priority demand.
- **Scarcity-layer Value Capture** — value is captured where substitutability is lowest, not necessarily where revenue share is highest.
- **Capacity Preemption** — prepayments and long-duration commitments reserve future scarce capacity.
- **Installed-base Flywheel** — deployed systems strengthen ecosystem, cash flow, and the next allocation round.
- **Rolling Bottleneck Cascade** — the binding constraint can move from logic to HBM, packaging, power, or another physical layer.
- **Cloud System-Stack Substitution** — cloud competition spans chips, HBM, interconnect, compiler/runtime, racks/pods, and workloads.
- **Anchor Compute Buyer Effect** — frontier model companies can become demand anchors for data centers, chips, and power.
- **AI Software Unit Economics** — application economics depend on inference, retrieval, tools, human review, and AI-cloud cost.
- **Task-to-Job Translation** — task exposure is not identical to employment loss.
