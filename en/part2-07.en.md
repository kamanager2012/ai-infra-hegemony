# Macro Essay 7: The Capital Supercycle, Clearing Risk, and Extreme Stress Tests

Chinese original: [part2-07.md](part2-07.md)

The depreciation wall is a near- and medium-term capital-cycle mechanism, not a direct forecast of the 2036 industry structure. See [Volume 30](vol-30.en.md) and the hyperscaler CapEx scope check. This essay focuses on duration mismatch, utilization, and cash-flow sensitivity.

## 1. The question this essay answers

The macro stress test asks three questions: which CapEx line items are actually comparable; how short-lived compute assets interact with long-lived buildings and power; and which combinations of utilization and unit-price decline damage buyer returns before seller economics weaken. Only company-specific disclosed figures enter the fact layer; aggregate scenario totals remain stress tests.

## 2. Evidence boundary

Historical versions used a four-year hardware depreciation template, a $215B mixed-year cloud-spending anchor, a $670–750B forward CapEx scenario, a fixed $115.5B depreciation estimate, and a nine-cell token price/usage matrix. These are **scenario arithmetic**, not company-disclosed AI P&L.

The current fact layer uses:
- company-specific CapEx/PPE and depreciation where disclosed;
- Microsoft FY25 D&A of **$34.153B** as one company-level anchor;
- NVIDIA FY26/FY27 seller economics and supply commitments;
- 2026 hyperscaler public guidance/TTM figures, kept in their own accounting scopes;
- observable changes in utilization, clearing prices, lead times, commitments, and CapEx guidance.

The stress test therefore asks whether workflow cash generation can cover asset duration and utilization—not whether one old aggregate spending number is “too large.”
## 3. Official profit and cash: the seller has already confirmed. The buyer is still building halls

The evidence is [Volume 1](vol-01.en.md). This essay opened Nvidia's 2026-02-25 press release and Microsoft's FY25 cash-flow page. Figures match the evidence. This essay is not a full 10-K audit.

Profit, cash, and supply commitments: [Volume 1](vol-01.en.md). This essay takes only two sentences: the seller almost never carries the hall; it leaves the hall to the buyer. Bidding for capacity looks at operating income, not net income lifted by marks. On the same Microsoft FY25 cash-flow statement, purchases $64,551M, depreciation, amortization, and other $34,153M. That depreciation row is the official depreciation this essay's stress test can take into the fact layer.

## 4. Four-cloud capex: rows that can be re-added. $215B and $750B cannot add to official

Each of the four reports its own. Line items and fiscal years do not align. Microsoft cuts on 30 June. The other three are calendar year. Figures from each IR / Exhibit 99.1. Scope: the already-checked `四云Capex口径核对.md` and [Volume 11](vol-11.en.md).

Mixed-year four-cloud: [Volume 8](vol-08.en.md) and `四云Capex口径核对.md`. This essay's stress test uses each firm's own row, not a mixed sum. Cannot be written as "four-cloud 2024 AI hardware capex = $215B," and cannot add historical stress-test material $670–$750B. Seller revenue and buyer PPE are different accounting objects and cannot be netted against one another.

## 4b. Why mixed-year cannot be divided by four

The step historical stress-test material most easily treats as "already happened" is taking $215B as AI hardware, then dividing by 4, getting $45B–$55B annualized depreciation. That step has three breakpoints. Break any one, and the breakeven revenue and token matrix after it break with it.

First: the numerator is not AI hardware. Section 4 already confirm it. $211.9–$213.9B is mixed-year group-wide equipment purchases. Fulfillment networks, Reality Labs, office buildings are inside. Dividing group-wide by 4 does not give "AI server depreciation." It amortizes warehouses, glasses, and conference rooms over four years too.

Second: the denominator is not a unified four years. On the same Microsoft cash-flow statement, purchases $64,551M, depreciation, amortization, and other $34,153M. If four-year straight line held, one firm's already-landed depreciation would not just equal one-fourth of that year's purchases. Civil works are even less four years. Bundling a 15-year power contract and a four-year GPU into one divisor is duration mixing itself, not accounting.

Third: the seller almost never enters this division. Nvidia FY26 itself spent only $6,042M on equipment, free cash $96,575M. If a stress test starts from "industry depreciation" and then treats seller profit as cash the industry has already recovered, it writes already-booked value capture as an investment not yet recovered. value capture is $193,737M. Investment is each buyer's own PPE. The two rows cannot be each other.

So this essay does not use $45B–$55B, and does not use $115.5B. Depreciation that can enter the fact layer is, for now, only this Microsoft row $34,153M. The other three names' D&A were not confirm in this pass's evidence. Leave them blank. Four-cloud total depreciation has no official total. Do not invent one.

## 5. The bubble's micro-mechanism is duration mixing

A bubble is not four characters, "priced too high." It bundles three durations into one narrative. First: a 2–4 year GPU generation, profit already confirmed on the seller. FY26 free cash $96,575M, Q1 FY27 one quarter of operating income $53,536M, last two quarters' gross margin back near 75%. Second: the buyer's IT depreciation of about 4 years. Microsoft FY25 depreciation, amortization, and other already reported $34,153M, clearly up from prior-year $22,287M; that is one firm's official depreciation, not a four-cloud total, and still less historical stress-test material $45B–$55B. Third: 15–20 year civil works and power contracts. Using the first duration's profit to prove the third duration's valuation is the error this essay takes apart.

Physical stickiness decides destocking is not symmetric. HBM lines and advanced packaging cannot be switched back to phones by the week; a high-power campus cannot be turned into apartments; a long power contract cannot be turned into spot. What can clear quickly is cabinets not yet racked and model-firm interfaces. So the first photograph of a "burst" should be shorter lead times and spot concessions, not a plant demolished. Fourth-layer assets will stay on the books, becoming a long liability or a moat after reconcentration, depending on the switches later, not on a one-week stock-price drawdown.

Commercially, usage sits in the workflow; unit price sits in open source and inference efficiency. The seller cannot control usage, yet locked its own shipments through prepayments. Profit confirms on the seller first. Risk bursts later on the buyer and the line. That is a classic capacity cycle. historical stress-test material wrote DeepSeek training about $5.576 million, KV compressed 93.3%, as the technical condition for a price cut. Those are historical stress-test values. This essay does not newly check training-cost page numbers. The mechanism can stay: a lower entry fee presses unit price. It does not automatically push usage to the matrix's +300% cell. Without that cell, historical stress-test material's "down 50% still earns $15B" does not exist.

Geopolitically, dual-track expansion may let two overhangs appear globally at once. Controls cut the return channel. Unilateral clearing is steeper. Sovereign-fund holdings were zero in historical stress-test material. Do not invent amounts. Analysis added leaves only the structure: if you only hold tech stocks of the first duration, you are on the same side as retail; if you hold power and licenses of the third duration, that is what may still be left in a stress test.

## 6. How prepayments move the stress-test clock onto the buyer

Three-month commitments from $95.2B to $119.0B, an add of $23.8B, about four-tenths of Q1 FY27 operating income $53,536M. This is not a bet under cash stress. It is the person with the highest profit adding one more bin of prepayments. Official text writes substantially all will be paid through fiscal year 2027; of quarter-end $119.0B, public footnotes have written the remainder pays through 2028–2031. Same note: in certain instances, these agreements are cancellable, able to be rescheduled, or adjustable. Not every dollar is irrevocable. Do not write it as already buying TSMC's whole factory dead.

Hypothesis from here: if seller cash flow, margin, order scale, and roadmap certainty remain strong, supply commitments can continue to rise. But rivals are not automatically priced out, and TSMC is not governed by a rule about "offending" one customer. Allocation depends on price, long-duration demand, prepayment, credit, utilization, diversification, geopolitics, and supplier bargaining power. A hard-landing stress test should therefore watch whether utilization and workflow revenue cover buyer depreciation **and** whether the next round of seller commitments slows, is rescheduled, or is cancelled.

Microsoft's comparison kills this. Net income $101,832M, operating cash $136,162M, PPE added $64,551M in a year, D&A already at $34,153M. The cloud is not unarmed. This hundred billion builds halls. NVIDIA FY26 net income $120,067M is earned selling the scarce slice itself. In the same year's free cash $96,575M, $41.1B went back to shareholders; it itself spent only $6,042M on equipment. A seller stress test can almost never do "capex crushes itself." A buyer stress test can, at any time.

## 7. Stress test: switches, not a fate table

historical stress-test material token matrix is scenario arithmetic, unverified. This essay does not invent a new unit price, a new call volume, or a new four-cloud total depreciation. Downgrade the matrix to a checklist: does your usage assumption match your unit-price assumption, and does it match already-taken depreciation. If you cannot answer, do not talk supercycle.

Switch A: soft landing. Usage keeps up with already-happened equipment purchases. The unit-price cut does not eat gross margin bare. Mixed-year 2025 four-cloud already added to about $354.0B, which says buyers were still adding then, not already cutting. On this path seller gross margin gives a little back. It does not collapse. The Agents mature period is historical stress-test material's narrative, not this essay's forecast.

Switch B: destocking. Usage parks on a low cell. Unit price falls clearly. What is testable is not "$750B becomes a bad debt" — that figure cannot be added — but shorter lead times, slower prepayment growth, second-hand cabinets appearing at a concession. HBM and advanced packaging show empty slots. Power contracts become idle watts. This is the buyer cracking first.

Switch C: clearing followed by reconcentration. Second-line model firms and seat-license software without workflow cash exit first. Quota and power contracts stay with clouds and makers who can wait. A bubble strengthens platform concentration. It does not prove AI is useless. 40% oligopoly is draft discussion weight. This essay assigns no probability.

Switch D: rates. historical stress-test material had no ten-year Treasury or tech implied vol. This essay invents no basis points. The mechanism holds: 4-year depreciation looks like growth investment in cheap money, and like leverage that must produce cash at once in expensive money. Mixed-year $211.9–$213.9B was made under some cost of funds. If 2026's un-addable $750B is stacked with a higher discount rate, the forecast table will collapse itself, without waiting for tokens down 50%. Writing a bubble entirely as a technology story or entirely as a speculation story both miss this term.

Counters: calling a stock-price drawdown a burst; treating $750B as already happened; writing mixed-year $215B as "AI hardware"; writing +300% / down 50% still earning as "a price cut is riskless."

## 8. Who exits first: order can be written. Headcount is not invented

By mechanism, not by shipment statistics.

The first wave is model firms and seat-license SaaS without workflow cash. historical stress-test material wrote margin walking from 85% toward 55%. That is a business-model assumption, not one firm's already-disclosed path. When usage cannot fill and unit price falls, this side first cuts people unrelated to delivery. Customer-side substitution projects stall. What the labor market will see is "AI-project layoffs," not "AI has already finished substituting."

The second wave is hardware resellers with no quota, piecing Ethernet clusters from spot. No prepayment, no queue. The moment lead times loosen, their inventory becomes a spot concession first.

The third wave is those among the clouds who filled 2026 spend to the un-addable $750B, while usage parked on a low cell. Depreciation precedes revenue. Microsoft has already reported one firm's D&A at $34,153M, which says D&A on buyer books is rising. It is not a narrative. Four-cloud total depreciation draft $45B–$55B is not promoted to official.

Only the fourth wave is Nvidia and the memory makers. Only when buyers cut prepayments, and advanced packaging and HBM show empty slots, will 71%–75% and Hynix 49% give back at once. Until then the seller is still the bidder with one year of $96,575M free cash and one quarter of $53,536M operating income. Plants and 15-year civil works almost never die. They only change owners.

This order is a reminder about "overweight physical scarcity." Overweighting assets after the fourth wave will look like a stupid long duration through the first three waves; in reconcentration they become what is still there. A stress test does not tell you which wave to stand on. It only tells you not to price the fourth wave with the first wave's stock price. Essay 7 and Volume 36 are two scales of the same thing: one writes the valuation clock, one writes who exits first.

The five paths that can interrupt this acceleration, the judgment already wrote: a real Western coalition; a cloud ASIC pulling training off; power jamming expansion dead; the East standing a second bid; a business split or a forced opening of CUDA. None of the five has been done. What has been done: supply commitments added to $119.0B; the investigation is there, a complaint is not. A fine is a cost. It does not change the bid. If those five are not done, the line walks the scarce slice past halfway. The window is these few years before next-generation first silicon is locked by commitments. A stress test that only watches the stock price will lose the window.

## 9. Testable indicators

Only public, non-inventable. Whether Nvidia supply commitments keep leaving $119.0B, or growth slows. Whether free cash still sits near the one-year $96,575M bin, and whether operating income is still read by the quarter, not by equity marks. Whether Microsoft PPE keeps leaving FY25 $64,551M, and D&A keeps leaving $34,153M. Whether Amazon net, Alphabet purchases, Meta equipment are still adding, or turning. Whether three-name 2026 guidance still parks near the evidence-confirm $200B / $175–$185B / $115–$135B, or later changes its mouth; a change of mouth is still group-wide, not an AI line item. Whether justice.gov shows a complaint is unrelated to this essay's depreciation, but what can change the rules is not a fine. HBM lead times, second-hand cabinet prints, public token prices: write them when there is a public series. If there is not, do not invent them.
