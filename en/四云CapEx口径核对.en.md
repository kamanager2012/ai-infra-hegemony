# Four-Cloud CapEx Scope Check

> Review/update date: **2026-08-28**  
> Purpose: keep Microsoft, Amazon, Alphabet, and Meta CapEx / PPE metrics separated by fiscal window, accounting line, and actual/guidance identity. Company-wide capital spending is not an official AI-hardware line item.

## Conclusion

1. The historical **US$215B** figure is not a directly comparable same-calendar-year, same-accounting-line “AI CapEx” total for the four companies.
2. A reconstructed mixed-window 2024 equipment/PPE scale is approximately **US$211.9B–213.9B**, depending mainly on Meta lease treatment; this is an analytical reconstruction, not a jointly reported company metric.
3. All four company figures are **company-wide CapEx / PPE measures**, not a disclosed subtotal for AI servers or GPUs.
4. As of August 2026, public investment scales remain extremely high, but the latest metrics still use different identities:
   - Microsoft: calendar-2026 reported CapEx expectation of approximately **US$175B** after lease-classification changes; management said underlying investment expectations were unchanged.
   - Alphabet: Q2 earnings-call full-year CapEx guidance of **US$195–205B**; this is company guidance reported through Reuters, not a statutory actual. Alphabet's Q2 2026 SEC materials separately report actual quarterly results.
   - Meta: Q2 full-year CapEx guidance of **US$130–145B**, including principal payments on finance leases.
   - Amazon: **US$169.007B** of TTM PPE net purchases actual through 2026-06-30.

These figures establish the direction and scale of infrastructure expansion. They **must not be mechanically summed into an official “2026 four-cloud AI hardware CapEx” total.**

## Prohibited extrapolations

- “Four-cloud 2024 AI CapEx = US$215B” as a formal company-reported fact.
- “2026 four-cloud AI hardware CapEx is officially US$670–750B.”
- Treating Alphabet's US$195–205B guidance as already-spent actual CapEx.
- Treating Amazon's TTM net PPE actual as directly comparable with calendar-year guidance from the other companies.
- Adding NVIDIA revenue to hyperscaler CapEx and interpreting the sum as unique global AI investment.

## Scope rule

The latest four-company metrics are useful for tracking **capital-expansion direction**. Any report table that places them together must preserve, at minimum:

- fiscal/calendar window;
- accounting line;
- actual vs. guidance / expectation;
- finance-lease treatment;
- whether the source is first-party or a secondary report of company guidance.

The figures become additive only after those identities are normalized to a common model. Until then, they remain a comparison set, not an official aggregate.
