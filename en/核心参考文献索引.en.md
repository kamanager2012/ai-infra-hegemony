## 9.6 School-scale AI tutoring and delayed learning

### Khanmigo two-year school experiment

- **Source:** Oreopoulos & Low, NBER Working Paper 35620, August 2026
- **URL:** https://www.nber.org/papers/w35620
- **Evidence grade:** **B — cluster randomized field experiment**
- **Supports:**
  - two-year cluster RCT across **18 Tennessee middle schools**;
  - assignment increased math achievement about **1.3 national percentile ranks per term**, approximately **0.06–0.08 SD over a school year**;
  - implied full-year active-participation effect about **0.14 SD**;
  - engagement was limited despite high initial trial: 96% tried Khanmigo, but median use was about one-third of practice days and ~17% of mistake sessions.
- **Boundary:**
  - the full learning effect is incremental to non-AI Khan Academy practice;
  - access alone guarantees engagement or learning;
  - school-scale achievement gains prove teacher replacement or system-level cost effectiveness.

### NUMI mastery experiment

- **Source:** Oreopoulos, Liut, Sungu & Low, NBER Working Paper 35621, August 2026
- **URL:** https://www.nber.org/papers/w35621
- **Evidence grade:** **B — randomized field experiment**
- **Supports:**
  - more than **6,000 middle-school students**;
  - AI vs CAL-only and mastery vs non-mastery randomization;
  - **one-week delayed assessment** on practiced and unpracticed material;
  - AI improved error recovery during practice;
  - delayed-learning evidence was more limited/context-dependent and strongest when AI was embedded in a mastery workflow.
- **Boundary:**
  - immediate AI-assisted correctness equals broad delayed transfer;
  - one-week delayed assessment proves persistent multi-year learning;
  - generic chatbot access is sufficient regardless of instructional design.

## 12. Citation discipline

1. For high-impact numbers, return to first-party or high-quality institutional sources.
2. Institutional forecasts must retain their identity as forecasts or estimates.
3. Company guidance must not be written as actual.
4. Contracted MW must not be written as energized MW.
5. Revenue share must not be converted into wafer or packaging share.
6. Stress tests must not be written as current facts.
7. If a later first-party disclosure conflicts with this page, the later disclosure wins and the [Core Evidence Ledger](核心证据台账.en.md) must be updated.
