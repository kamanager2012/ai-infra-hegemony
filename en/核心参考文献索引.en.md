## 12. Citation discipline

1. For high-impact numbers, return to first-party or high-quality institutional sources.
2. Institutional forecasts must retain their identity as forecasts or estimates.
3. Company guidance must not be written as actual.
4. Contracted MW must not be written as energized MW.
5. Revenue share must not be converted into wafer or packaging share.
6. Stress tests must not be written as current facts.
7. If a later first-party disclosure conflicts with this page, the later disclosure wins and the [Core Evidence Ledger](核心证据台账.en.md) must be updated.
