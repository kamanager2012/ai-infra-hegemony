# Macro Essay 8: U.S.–China Compute Competition and the 2036 Endgame

Chinese original: [part2-08.md](part2-08.md)

China stack, three layers: [Volume 12](vol-12.md). Endgame switches: [Volume 10](vol-10.md) and [Volume 15](vol-15.md). This essay does not reopen prospectus retrieval or the scenario tree. This essay only collects: the China market cannot save the globe; the endgame table does not enter the facts.

## 1. The question this essay answers

Export controls have accelerated the formation of a China-side parallel stack, but the relevant question is system-level closure rather than a single “domestic substitution” percentage. Smartphone, PC/general computing, and frontier AI require different combinations of logic, memory, packaging, interconnect, software, and scale. The CXMT prospectus does not disclose an HBM product line, and historical 40/30/15/15 scenario weights are not part of the formal framework.

The current layered conclusion is narrower: China has stronger parallel supply in smartphones and parts of consumer/general computing, while frontier-AI equivalence still depends on advanced logic, HBM-class memory, advanced packaging, high-speed interconnect, compiler/runtime maturity, and deployment scale.

## 2. Evidence boundary

Historical versions mixed unverified Ascend bandwidth, CANN rewrite-rate claims, future HBM plans, single-point foundry shares, tool prices, fab costs, and fixed 2036 probabilities. Those values do not enter the current fact layer unless separately supported by first-party or high-quality institutional evidence.

The current baseline is:
- TSMC 2025 HPC share **58%**, advanced packaging + testing slightly above **10%**;
- 2026 Q1 foundry revenue share: TSMC ~**72%**, Samsung ~**6.5%**, SMIC ~**5.1%**;
- CXMT filing: DDR/LPDDR product lines disclosed, no HBM product row;
- YMTC: NAND/SSD, not HBM;
- DeepSeek-V3: **2.788M H800 GPU-hours** as an algorithmic-efficiency anchor;
- China-side accelerator, interconnect, and compiler/runtime ecosystems are developing, but system-level frontier substitution still has to close across all scarce layers.

The formal future framework is S1–S4 with H1–H17—not fixed 40/30/15/15 probabilities.
## 3. Mechanism (physical / commercial / geopolitical)

### 3.1 Three layers are not one layer

The phone layer's scarcity is a slice of leading-edge logic and residual LPDDR. Apple locks its own phone logic. TSMC 2025 platforms: HPC 58% (up 48% in a year), smartphone 29%. Domestic shipments, brands, and channels are still there. Kirin plus SMIC is this layer's parallel supply. CXMT commodity DRAM can supply this layer. Paying a memory tax, down-speccing, slower replacement cannot save the other two layers.

The PC layer grabs the same residual DRAM, consumer SSDs, gaming cards. NVIDIA itself has written Blackwell and Ada supply limited. Domestic OEMs are still there. They have no accelerator stack of their own, and no CoWoS queue. Still x86, Windows, discrete GPUs. Residual thinner. Unresolved: it cannot solve DIMMs, disks, and cards being sucked into cabinets.

The AI layer's scarcity is packaging, HBM, cabinet fabric, the compiler/runtime stack. A domestic parallel stack is for people who cannot buy a cabinet. It cannot take a Western photoresist. Matching phone share or PC shipments to AI cabinets is the wrong layer. Residual: [Volume 5](vol-05.md). Cabinet count times spec: [Volume 16](vol-16.md). Domestic commodity bits follow the residual river's international price. They cannot fill the half of bits inside the cabinet.

### 3.2 CXMT prospectus full text: 0 mentions of HBM

The main source is the SSE 2026-05-20 registration draft. The evidence group used pdftotext to read through and search HBM / 高带宽内存, hits 0. Products only write the DDR series, the LPDDR series. 2025 main business: DDR 1,953,128.56 万元, 31.87%; LPDDR 4,070,354.50 万元, 66.43%; other products and services 104,054.15 万元, 1.70%; main-business total 6,127,537.21 万元. DDR plus LPDDR 98.30% of main business. No HBM row.

Three fundraising projects: memory wafer-manufacturing volume-line technical upgrade 75.00 亿元, DRAM memory technical upgrade 180.00 亿元, DRAM forward-looking technology research and development 90.00 亿元. Intended proceeds 295.00 亿元. The forward-looking project's original text writes forward-looking technology adapted to future advanced DRAM, construction period written 2026–2028. The two characters HBM do not appear. 90 亿 is a DRAM forward-looking basket, not a standalone HBM line.

Capacity: the prospectus writes Hefei and Beijing, three 12-inch DRAM fabs in all, Omdia scope China first, global fourth. Full-text search 35 万 / 三十五万 hits R&D-expense decimals, not monthly capacity. 350k wafers/month is a SemiAnalysis outlook, carried by Korean media and 21 Jingji. It does not enter the fact layer. The 21 Jingji 2026-06-25 piece carrying SemiAnalysis also writes: the prospectus discloses no dedicated HBM project, and does not mention HBM. That matches the full-text search.

CXMT listed on STAR on 2026-07-27, short name 长鑫科技, code 688825. The listing announcement confirms the stock landed. It does not change the prospectus product table. Once the international price holds, makers sell first to the high bidder. What the books sell is already-risen DDR / LPDDR. Following the price on the slice that makes money is not opening a new HBM river against the three majors. Allocation blocks domestic bits from flowing out. It is not already nursing CXMT to maturity. "Domestic CXMT exists, therefore PCs and AI have bits" does not hold. PCs take residual DIMMs. AI does not get equivalent stacks. Still unresolved.

Maker books: [Volume 5](vol-05.md). The three majors stand on the cabinet side. CXMT follows the commodity-bit slice whose international price is already standing. It is not rerouting the global HBM river.

### 3.3 YMTC is NAND third, not an HBM spare

The YMTC prospectus (SSE 2026-08-21 filing draft) official sentence is among the world's first to ship 200-layer-and-above 3D NAND, not the 267 layers media commonly use. January–March 2026 operating revenue 470.42 亿元, net profit attributable 333.79 亿元. TrendForce scope: sales amount and units both global third, China first. Fundraising 330 亿元: volume-line technical upgrade 208.00 亿元, R&D 122.00 亿元. Full text has no official monthly capacity in the 10,000-wafer class. If HBM appears, it is explaining an industry noun, not a YMTC product line. YMTC eats NAND / SSD price-ups. It can fill consumer disks and a slice of enterprise SSD. It cannot fill HBM, and cannot fill the server-DDR5 half of bits.

### 3.4 SMIC 5.1% cannot take a Western photoresist

TrendForce 2026-06-12 official piece: 2026 Q1 global top-ten foundry combined revenue $47.95 billion. TSMC about $35.86 billion, share expanded to 72%, the driver written as AI-server GPU / xPU. Samsung Foundry about $3.2 billion, share 6.5%. SMIC $2.51 billion, share 5.1%, the pull written as TV brands and notebook ODMs pulling inventory forward, plus 8-inch price-ups, not 3nm training cabinets. Public volume fabs that can do top-tier AI logic are still mostly TSMC, with Samsung as a parallel line. SMIC 5.1% cannot take a Western photoresist. Apple, AMD, Qualcomm handing GDS to a China-controlled fab pass the export-control and foreign-direct-product-rule gate, not a yield race. Even if Eastern capacity rises, it is a parallel bid, not a substitute for these names' seat at TSMC.

Cloud ASICs: [Volume 4](vol-04.md). This slice of SMIC foundry revenue cannot take the 3nm-plus-HBM line. Huawei is a buyer. A high price inside the restricted domestic market is value capture redirected away from NVIDIA, not a rewrite of global platform value capture. Huaqiangbei: [Essay 5](part2-05.md).

### 3.5 The near gate has already fallen. The far gate is still on non-silicon

The near gate is administrative lock. See [Volume 12](vol-12.md). The 10-K itself writes China data center effectively foreclosed. The far gate is non-silicon: if domestic advanced packaging and HBM-grade bits can still volume enough to hold a training cabinet, administrative lock is enough to keep the loop closed; if not, only the phone layer remains. Prospectus 0 mentions of HBM, SMIC 5.1%, packaging tight: the far gate is not open now. National-security documents: [Volume 8](vol-08.md). The documents only prove it was written as national security. They do not prove a second packaging and HBM line has already been copied at home.

### 3.6 The East's second bid has not stood

The five paths: [Volume 8](vol-08.md). A mature China-side path would require domestic advanced packaging and HBM-grade memory at sufficient scale for frontier systems, plus enough long-duration demand to compete credibly for incremental foundry and memory capacity. The prospectus is still selling DDR / LPDDR. Foundry share is parked on TVs and notebooks. A second global-scale resource bid has not yet been demonstrated. The current China stack is driven primarily by constrained access, domestic demand, strategic autonomy, and improving system capability rather than a demonstrated global cost advantage over CUDA.

## 4. Scenarios, counterarguments, and testable indicators

The endgame table does not enter the facts. The four branches are used as switches. No frequencies.

Switch one: incumbent system-stack concentration strengthens. The unit of control moves further upward from chips to certified systems and interfaces. Test: whether TSMC HPC continues to outgrow smartphones, whether customer concentration changes, and whether supply commitments continue to rise.

Switch two: China-stack closure. A second compiler/runtime ecosystem materially lowers switching cost and frontier workloads run at competitive scale. Test: whether CXMT's next prospectus or annual report shows an HBM product row and an HBM revenue row; whether YMTC still only writes 200-layer-and-above NAND; whether SMIC share is still pulled by TVs and notebooks; public MFU comparisons of MoE on CANN clusters.

Switch three: power jams expansion first. Energization constraint is a real fork. Mixed-year 2025 four-cloud equipment purchases are still adding, not already jammed. Test: whether new campuses are built and energized, or built waiting on a feeder.

Switch four: cloud system stacks pull a material share of training away from incumbent systems. Public next-generation products exist, but industry-wide training substitution remains incomplete. Test: whether an official training share appears, not only an inference shunt.

Switch five: hard landing. Buyers cut group-wide capex. Mixed-year 2025 is still adding, not already cut. Test: whether lead times, prepayment terms, and second-hand cabinets all soften at once.

Historical 40/30/15/15 weights, 1,000× demand shocks, 80% concentration points, and single-fab cost estimates remain outside the formal scenario model. Their use is to distinguish branches, not to supply a 2036 point forecast. Picking the endgame you like first and then finding numbers becomes a wish list. First see the power, packaging, stack, and cash in hand, then pick a branch.

Counters: writing 910C as already equivalent to NVL72; writing DeepSeek as already lifting all process blockades; writing the CXMT listing as already volume HBM; writing 350k wafers/month as the prospectus; writing SMIC 5.1% as able to take a Western photoresist; treating 40% as a plan target.

The world is not blowing open. It is an upgrade parked on the next-lower node. Phones and PCs pay a memory tax. Rules are set by one firm. So-called order is the price of the gate and the identity of the gatekeeper. Gatekeepers are power, packaging, CUDA / CANN, and licenses, not an abstract East and West. Whoever can check the gatekeeper is still inside the method. Whoever starts writing civilizational rise and fall has left the range public files can hold.

For firms: do not sign a full set of unchecked spend forecasts for an oligopoly narrative. First see whether you can live on workflow cash in a cell where unit price falls and usage may not double, then decide on prepayments. An ASIC is a private cost-down. Open source is a public cost-down. Neither substitutes for power and packaging. For nations: power licenses and leading-edge process must be written in the same file; algorithm leverage is real leverage when a single point cannot be copied; dual-track spare parts done now are cheaper than done in the endgame year. For individuals: all four branches demand the same underlying capability, being able to say power, heat, packaging, and depreciation clearly. Compound training does not fill a photoresist gate, and does not fill 51% of bits. A cognitive leap does not change the bid.
