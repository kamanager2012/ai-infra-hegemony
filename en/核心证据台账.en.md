## Usage rules

- A/B evidence may enter “already observed” discussion, but B must retain institutional identity and scope.
- C must remain labeled as sell-side / supply-chain / channel estimates.
- D must remain labeled hypothesis, scenario, model, or stress test.
- A number may not be upgraded simply because it is repeated across chapters.
- When a first-party source supersedes a C estimate, upgrade only the supported portion.
- When a later first-party disclosure contradicts an estimate, discard the old value and update every dependent chapter.
- 2030/2036 single-point ratios should normally be replaced by scenario ranges rather than pseudo-precision.
