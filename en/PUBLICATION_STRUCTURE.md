# Publication Architecture — The Age of AI Infrastructure Hegemony

> This page defines the **final reading architecture**. Existing `vol-01`–`vol-40` and `part2-01`–`part2-08` files remain stable research-module IDs for evidence tracing and Git history. They are **not the final book chapter order**.

## Why the architecture changes

The corpus was produced in layers: the first twenty volumes establish the main mechanisms and scenarios; the later twenty deepen NVIDIA, HBM, advanced packaging, power, capital, labor, and other topics; the eight macro essays add engineering quantities, BOM logic, capital/depreciation analysis, and cross-volume synthesis.

These layers are not simple duplicates, but presenting them as “40 volumes + 8 more essays” creates repeated entry points. The publication edition therefore keeps the research modules while reorganizing the reading flow.

## Editorial rules

- One factual table has one primary home; other chapters link to it rather than reproducing it.
- A mechanism is fully defined once. Later chapters add evidence, boundaries, counterarguments, or falsification signals.
- A deep-dive chapter must add unique engineering, quantitative, or evidentiary content.
- Macro essays become synthesis material inside the relevant publication chapters rather than an appended second book.
- Sources move with the factual claims they support.
- Historical chat-style line breaks do not survive into the publication layer.
- Technical units retain standard industry notation: TB, GB, GHz, MHz, kW, MW, GW, MWh, TWh.

## Research layer vs. publication layer

The repository keeps two layers:

- **Research layer:** 40 volumes + 8 macro essays for traceability, independent revision, and Git diffs.
- **Publication layer:** approximately 32 chapters in four parts for continuous reading, PDF/e-book production, and public release.

Existing volume numbers remain stable until the publication layer is generated.
