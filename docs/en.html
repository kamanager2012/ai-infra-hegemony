AI Buyers Are Repricing the Stack

Who gets advanced logic, HBM, packaging, power — and what is left for everyone else?

The central claim is not that one chip wins forever. It is that AI competition has moved upward into the system stack, while scarce capacity, alternative stacks, algorithmic efficiency, and financing determine who captures the next unit of compute — and whether today’s bottlenecks persist, migrate, or reverse.
