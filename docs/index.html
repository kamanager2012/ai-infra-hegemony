EVIDENCE DISCIPLINE

Revenue mix ≠ wafer allocation.
Economic direction is not physical customer allocation.

Secured ≠ energized ≠ utilized.
Power and LPS retain delivery-state identity.

Target ≠ funded capital.
Guarantee exposure, CapEx and independent demand remain separate.

Substitution ≠ autonomy.
Production-grade deployment and frontier closure are different tests.
