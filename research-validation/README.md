# Phase II · Empirical Validation & Scenario Calibration

This directory is the **independent validation layer** created after the 32-chapter formal report was frozen. It is not part of the frozen publication text and does not change the formal report's **August 28, 2026 evidence cutoff**.

Its purpose is not to keep polishing the publication. It exists to test the report's highest-impact mechanisms that still require historical, causal, or prospective calibration.

**Canonical research language: English.** All Phase II analytical files are authored in English. Any Chinese version is a derived translation and must not create, remove, reorder, or redefine evidence identities, denominators, state labels, causal mechanisms, thresholds, or conclusions independently of the English canonical text.

## Work order

1. **P0-1 Historical Clearing Backtest** — test the five Chapter 24 clearing-signal families;
2. **P0-2 AI Demand Elasticity Calibration** — estimate empirical price/usage response using reproducible model-traffic data;
3. **P0-3 Verified Workflow Economics** — operationalize cost per verified workflow outcome and durable workflow ROI;
4. **P0-4 Macro-Financial Transmission** — connect rates, credit, equity support, project finance, and refinancing to the capital cycle;
5. **P1 Geopolitical Scenario Layer** — make Taiwan continuity, export controls, alliance structure, and China closure explicit state variables;
6. **P1 Conditional Timeline** — convert fixed-year language into condition → milestone → contrary-signal logic;
7. **P2 Downstream Empirical Chains** — maintain auditable Labor / Education / Physical-AI transmission chains;
8. **P1 Demand-Side Reflexivity** — own the labor-income → consumption → enterprise cash generation → AI monetization loop, its clocks, and its demand-side observables.
9. **P1 Capital-to-Cash Bridge** — perform the required-cash-versus-contracted-revenue division per GW-year from already-graded anchors, and maintain it as a reproducible artifact.
10. **P1 Cost-per-VSWO Break-Even Surface** — state the dimensionless conditions under which a claimed workflow effect survives the P0-3 closure identity.
11. **P1 Time-to-Power Energization Surface** — compute the announced → energized → utilized → revenue-bearing schedule as a conditional surface over published lead-time ranges, including the critical-path overlap credit.
12. **P1 Ramp-Period Funding Gap** — put the required-cash floor on the energization clock and compute the cumulative funding requirement, its peak, and the crossover.
13. **P1 Chain Concentration Amplification** — quantify how far an origin shock travels down a fixed-obligation financing chain, at what point each link breaches, and which channel is transmitting.
14. **P1 Demand-Side Composition & Incidence** — put the observed adoption rates on a common denominator, and show that the incidence question reduces to one ratio that bounds the demand effect rather than freeing it.

## Freeze boundary

- Phase II may use post-August-28 research methods, historical-source organization, and later observations;
- those observations remain **research-side validation**, not formal-report facts;
- Phase II does not write results back into the 32 chapters unless an explicit reopen is recorded;
- any formal-text reopen must record the reason, affected chapters, evidence identity, and cutoff treatment.

Its justification is recorded in `20` §0.

Reproducible via `research-validation/scripts/recompute_ramp_gap.py`; no new numeric identities; no formal-text change.
- **Chain Concentration Amplification:** **v0.1.** New research-side layer quantifying the variable `11` names and cannot compute. Reads only `11`'s chain and `04C`'s observed anchors — no new numeric identity. At the observed concentration (CoreWeave ≈ **77%** of Core Scientific H1 2026 revenue) the ratio is simultaneously the amplification factor and the residual: a total origin shutdown still leaves the downstream link **23%** of its revenue, and surviving one requires **5.00×** current coverage (= 1.15 / 0.23). The shock **attenuates** outward, so the link nearest the origin absorbs the largest fraction. The layer's sharpest content is that the mechanism is not one thing: **proportional pass-through** (variable obligation, damage drifts as `c x`) versus **counterparty step** (fixed obligation, damage is zero then `c` at once, up to **5.00×** larger at the moment of breach) — and `04C`'s recorded **lockbox** on CoreWeave revenues is evidence for the step, which makes **CoreWeave's** breach point the decisive quantity rather than Core Scientific's. The distinguishing signature is directly observable in the next downstream filing. Finally, concentration and immediacy point at **different links**: the 77%-concentrated link has nothing due 2026–2028 and ring-fenced cash, while the link facing the wall has **US$6.1B due in remainder-2026** and committed undrawn liquidity covering the 2026 wall **1.64×** but the 2026–2027 wall only **0.85×**. Reproducible via `research-validation/scripts/recompute_chain_amplification.py`; no formal-text change.
- **Demand-Side Composition & Incidence:** **v0.1.** New research-side layer giving the demand leg the arithmetic `14` deliberately leaves out. No anchors of its own; every rate is read from `14` §2. Reproducible via `research-validation/scripts/recompute_demand_composition.py`; no new numeric identities; `10` §6D unchanged; no formal-text change.
